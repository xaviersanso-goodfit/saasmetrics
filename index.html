How the figures here are defined

MRR normalized monthly subscription revenue. Each recurring line is amortized to net divided by term in months and counted at a month end when live.
ARR annualized run rate. MRR times 12.
ARPA average revenue per account. MRR divided by active customers.
MRR movements split each customer month on month into new, expansion, reactivation, contraction and churn, per the cheat sheet definitions.
Billed (total) the invoiced amount, ex VAT, in the month the invoice is dated. This is cash-basis billings, not recognized revenue.
Recognized revenue earned in the month: recurring fees spread straight line across the contract term from the start month, one-off fees taken in the billing month.
Deferred the in-month movement, billed minus recognized. Positive adds to the deferred revenue balance, negative releases it. The running total of a client's deferred column is its closing deferred balance.
